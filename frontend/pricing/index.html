Pricing questions

More answers on the FAQ page.

What does bring your own key cost me?

Jivika charges nothing on the Free plan. Your model provider bills you for the requests you make, at its published rates. Google Gemini and Groq offer free tiers, and OpenRouter offers some free models that you can enter in Settings, all subject to the provider's limits. You can also try every tool on the shared demo model, with a small hourly limit, before adding a key.

Is my key safe?

Your key is kept in your browser, in session storage by default or local storage if you choose to remember it. It is sent with each request in a header so the server can call your provider, and it is never stored or logged on our servers. You can remove it from Settings at any time.
