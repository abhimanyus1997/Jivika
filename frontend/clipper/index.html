Jivika Clipper

Send any job to Jivika in one click.

Some job sites, including Naukri and at times LinkedIn, block server imports. Jivika Clipper is a small browser extension that reads the job you are already viewing and opens it in Jivika, ready for a match, ATS check, cover letter or interview prep. It can also import your LinkedIn profile and fill application forms for you to check.

What it does

When you click the Jivika button on a job page, the Clipper reads the title, company, location, experience, salary and description, shows you a preview, and opens the Jivika page you choose with the job description filled in.

Privacy

It reads only the current tab, and only when you click. It sends nothing to our servers: the job travels to the app in the URL fragment, which browsers never send to a server. Each clip opens Jivika in a new tab. It has no analytics and no remote code.

Install on Chrome, Edge or Brave

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Download jivika-clipper.zip and extract it to a folder you will keep.

  2. 2

    Open the extensions page

    Go to chrome://extensions in Chrome, edge://extensions in Edge or brave://extensions in Brave.

  3. 3

    Turn on Developer mode

    Switch on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.

  4. 4

    Clip a job

    Pin Jivika Clipper to the toolbar, open a job posting and click the button. Choose where to send it: Match in Jivika, ATS check, Cover letter or Interview prep.

Import your LinkedIn profile

Open your own profile on LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/your-name) and click the Clipper. Instead of the job buttons you see two choices:

  • Import to my profile opens the Jivika Profile page with a preview of your experience, education, skills, languages, certifications, projects and publications. Choose what to keep, then merge it with your profile or replace it. You can undo the import.
  • Review my LinkedIn fills the LinkedIn review with your headline, About section and recent roles, ready for a review.

Before reading, the Clipper scrolls through the page and opens the "see more" sections so nothing is cut off, then returns you to where you were. If it cannot tell the sections apart, Jivika offers to build your profile from the page text with your model, which leaves out anything the text does not contain. Recommendations are never read.

Fill applications

On an application form (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable and most other forms), open the Clipper and choose Fill this application. It fills your name, contact details, links, current role, education and the answers you saved in Jivika, such as notice period or work authorization.

  • Filled fields get a green outline; required fields it could not fill get an amber one, so you can see what is left for you.
  • It never attaches files, never submits the form and never moves to the next step. On multi-step forms such as Workday, it fills the step you are on.
  • Diversity questions (gender, ethnicity, veteran and disability status) are answered with "Prefer not to say" or "Decline to answer" unless you choose otherwise in Jivika.

Filling needs a connected profile, see below. Check every answer before you submit.

Connect to Jivika

Open Jivika Settings and find the Jivika Clipper section. It shows whether the Clipper is installed and which version. Fill in your application answers, then choose Connect: Jivika copies your profile and answers into the extension's own storage in this browser. Choose Send profile again after you change them, or Disconnect to remove the copy.

Connecting works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers, and only from the Jivika app. Nothing is sent to Jivika's servers.

Check your version

The current version is 0.2.0. The Clipper shows its version at the bottom of its window, Jivika Settings shows the installed version, and Jivika tells you when a clip comes from an older copy.

  1. 1

    Download the new version

    Download jivika-clipper.zip and extract it over the folder you loaded before (or into a new folder).

  2. 2

    Reload it

    On the extensions page, choose the reload button on the Jivika Clipper card. If you used a new folder, remove the old copy and load the new folder with Load unpacked.

  3. 3

    Confirm

    Open the Clipper: the footer should read version 0.2.0. Your settings are kept.

Permissions

  • Read the current tab when you click (activeTab, scripting). The Clipper reads or fills only the tab you clicked it on, at that moment.
  • Storage. Keeps its settings and, when you connect, your profile and answers, in this browser only.
  • Talk to the Jivika app (externally connectable). Only pages on jivika.abhimanyu.fyi and your own computer (localhost) can ask for its version. Connecting and disconnecting work from jivika.abhimanyu.fyi, or from a local copy of Jivika only after you save its address in the Clipper options. No other site can reach it.
  • Optional per-site access, off by default. If you turn on "Show a Fill with Jivika button on this site", your browser asks for access to that one site. You can remove sites, and their access, in the Clipper options at any time.

Bookmarklet

Prefer not to install an extension? Drag this link to your bookmarks bar, then click the bookmark on a job page. It sends the job to Job match, or on your LinkedIn profile, imports it to your Jivika profile. To update the bookmarklet, drag the link again and replace the old bookmark.
