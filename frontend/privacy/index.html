Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information %VITE_COMPANY_NAME% (“Jivika”, “we”, “us”), operated by Abhimanyu Singh in India, handles when you use this website and the Jivika app, where it is kept and what control you have over it. In short: your key and your career data stay in your browser, and we keep as little as we can.

What is stored in your browser

The app stores the following in your browser’s local storage or session storage on your device. We do not receive a copy of this storage.

  • Your model API key. Kept in session storage by default, which is cleared when you close the tab, or in local storage if you choose to remember it on this device.
  • Your resume and profile, analysis results, scores and their history, cover letters and tracked applications.
  • Preferences such as your chosen provider and model, and interface settings.

You can delete all of this at any time with Clear all local data in the app’s Settings, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What passes through our server

When you run a tool, the app sends the relevant content, such as your resume text or a job description, to our server along with your API key in a request header. The server uses the key to call the model provider you selected and returns the response to your browser.

  • Your API key is used only for that request. It is not stored, logged or returned by the server.
  • Resume files and the content of your requests are processed only to produce a response and are not kept after the request ends. They are never written to a database. Uploads larger than 1 MB may be buffered in a temporary file that is deleted when the request finishes.

Jivika Clipper browser extension

The optional Jivika Clipper extension and bookmarklet read only the tab you click them on, and only when you click. They send nothing to our servers: a clipped job or LinkedIn profile opens in the app inside the URL fragment, which browsers never send to a server, and the app removes it from the address bar after reading it.

  • When you connect it in Settings, the app copies your profile and the application answers you entered into the extension’s own storage in your browser, so it can fill forms. Disconnect, or Clear all local data, removes that copy; removing the extension does too.
  • Autofill writes only into the application form you choose, when you ask it to. It never attaches files or submits the form, and it does not send what it fills to us.
  • Per-site access is off by default. If you turn on the Fill with Jivika button for a site, your browser asks for access to that one site, and you can remove it at any time.

Demo model

If you have not added a key, tools run on a shared demo model provided by Groq using a key held on our server. Your request content is sent to Groq in the same way as with your own key. To apply fair-use limits, the server counts demo requests per IP address for up to an hour; the count holds no request content and expires on its own.

Job listings

Job search sends your keywords and location to our server, which fetches matching listings from third-party job boards and company career pages and returns them to your browser. Listings come from those sources, are shown as-is with links to the original, and are not verified by Jivika. Optional research sources fetch publicly available listings on demand and may be disabled. Search terms may be cached briefly on the server to reduce repeat requests; your resume is never sent for job search, and the quick fit score is computed in your browser.

Model providers

The model provider you choose, such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral AI, DeepSeek, xAI or OpenRouter, receives the content sent in each request and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. You have the relationship with that provider through your own account and key. Please review their policies before you use them with personal information.

Where data is processed

Our server and the model provider you choose may be located outside India. By using a provider, you understand that the content you send is processed in the countries where that provider operates.

Waitlist

If you join the Pro waitlist, we store your email address, the plan you are interested in, the page you signed up from and the time you signed up in a database on our server. We use it solely to contact you about Jivika Pro. We do not sell it or share it for marketing. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL%, and you can unsubscribe from any email we send. We keep your entry until Pro launches and we have contacted you about it, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Server logs

Like most web services, our server and hosting provider may record minimal request metadata, such as IP address, time, requested path and response status, to operate the service, prevent abuse and fix problems. To limit abuse of the waitlist, the IP addresses of waitlist requests are also held in server memory for rate limiting. They are never written to disk and are discarded when the server restarts or newer entries replace them. These logs do not include your API key or the content of your requests.

Cookies, analytics and ads

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and other assets are served from our own domain, so loading the site does not contact third parties.
