Feature details

Job match

See how well you fit a role before you apply.

Paste any job description and Jivika compares it with your resume. The score blends the model's judgement with a deterministic keyword check, so it is harder to inflate.

  • Matched and missing skills, side by side
  • Strengths, gaps, challenges and opportunities for the role
  • A step-by-step plan to close the gaps

CV review

Find out what a screening system sees in your CV.

Get an ATS-style score with specific feedback for each section, instead of generic advice.

  • Section-by-section feedback
  • Rewritten bullet points with stronger outcomes
  • Scores tracked over time on your dashboard

Application tracker

Keep your search organised as it grows.

Record every application with its company, role and status. Your progress feeds the dashboard so you can see what is working.

  • A status for every application, from applied to offer
  • Application trends on the dashboard
  • Stored in your browser, not on our servers

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on the FAQ page.

Is Jivika free?

Yes. The Free plan includes every tool. You add your own model key and pay your provider directly for usage, and several providers offer free tiers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Open the app, add a key in Settings and upload your resume.

Where is my data stored?

Your resume, analyses and applications are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not saved on our servers, and you can clear them from Settings at any time.

What is Jivika Pro?

Pro will provide hosted AI so you do not need your own key. It is not available yet. Join the waitlist to hear when it launches; pricing will be announced then.
