01Find

Find roles worth applying to.

Turn your resume into a structured profile once, then tailor it to every job. Tailoring rewords and reorders what is already there; it never invents experience.

  • Profile and tailored resumes

    A structured profile, resume themes and a tailored version per job, with every version kept and downloadable as a PDF.

  • ATS checker

    See how applicant tracking systems read your resume: parsing, sections, formatting and missing keywords.

  • Cover letters

    Letters that use only facts from your resume, with templates and PDF export.

    Questions tailored to a specific role, with feedback on the answers you write.

    Python, JavaScript and SQL problems that run in your browser, with hints, solutions and a code review.

  • CV and LinkedIn review

    Section-by-section CV scores with rewritten bullets, plus headline and About rewrites for LinkedIn.

Privacy

Your career details stay with you.

There is no account to create and no database of resumes on our side. What you add to Jivika lives in your browser.

Read the privacy policy

  • Stored locally. Your resume, analyses and applications are kept in your browser's storage, and you can clear them at any time.

  • Keys never saved. Your model key stays in your browser and is sent only with each request. Our server does not store or log it.

  • You choose the provider. The model provider you pick processes your prompts under its own terms.

  • Open and self-hostable. Read every line on GitHub, or run Jivika yourself, including with local models.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on the FAQ page, or get in touch.

Is Jivika free?

Yes. Every tool is free. You can start on the shared demo model straight away, then add your own model key and pay your provider directly for usage. Several providers offer free tiers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Open the app, upload your resume and start; add a key in Settings whenever you like.

Where is my data stored?

Your resume, analyses and applications are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not saved on our servers, and you can export or clear them from Settings at any time.

Can I self-host Jivika?

Yes. Jivika is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, and the code is on GitHub. Self-hosted installs can also use local models.

What is Jivika Pro?

Pro will provide hosted AI so you do not need your own key. It is not available yet. Join the waitlist to hear when it launches; pricing will be announced then.
