How to use Jivika Clipper
  1. Open a job page on LinkedIn, Naukri or any careers site, or your LinkedIn profile.
  2. Click Jivika Clipper in the toolbar.
  3. Pick Match, ATS check, Cover letter or Interview prep. On a profile, pick Import.

Tip: pin Jivika Clipper from the puzzle-piece menu so it is always one click away.
